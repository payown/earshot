## Review notes

```text
Earshot is an accessibility-first podcast player. No account or sign-in is required.

To review the app:

1. Open Library and choose Add Podcast.
2. Search for a podcast or enter this RSS feed URL:
   https://pinecast.com/feed/technically-working
3. Select the show and play any episode.
4. Earshot Plus is available from Settings > Upgrade to Earshot Plus. The free tier permits up to 10 podcast subscriptions. Monthly, Yearly, and Lifetime purchases remove that limit.
5. Restore Purchases is available in Settings.
6. Optional consumable tips are available under Settings > Help & About > Leave a Tip.

VoiceOver is supported throughout the app. Background audio and lock-screen controls become available while an episode is playing.

No login, account, special hardware, or backend credentials are required.
```
