# Earshot 1.2.2 release notes

Candidate: version 1.2.2, build 264. App Store submission is being prepared.

## App Store “What’s New” copy

- Set a custom playback speed, including precise choices such as 0.98×. With VoiceOver, double-tap Playback speed to open the options.
- Choose a sleep timer that restarts when you interact with the app, including VoiceOver navigation.
- Unfollow a podcast directly from Podcast Settings, with confirmation before removal.
- Hear “Mini player” before the current episode title so it is easier to distinguish from your episode list.
- Play unheard episodes from a folder across multiple shows, oldest first, with a saved place so you can continue later.
- Give followed podcasts personal display names to make your Library easier to navigate.
- Move to the previous or next episode in your Queue, with optional wrapping through remaining episodes.
- Find playback controls and Library actions more easily with updated organization and VoiceOver hints.
- See clearer download progress, requests waiting for Wi-Fi, and failed downloads.
- Keep your Inbox within each podcast’s episode and age limits after refreshing, including feeds with no new episodes.
- Adjust podcast settings with less unnecessary background and interface work.
- Resume your saved episode more reliably with system playback controls after a pause in the background.

Removing episodes from Inbox now respects Delete downloads when done.

This update also adds an offline guide explaining how Inbox, Queue, downloads, and automatic download settings work together.

## Release details

- Folder runs are device-local and separate from the normal Queue. They include stored episodes and older episodes still available from publishers’ feeds; they cannot recover unavailable archives.
- Inbox limits dismiss older Inbox entries. Download cleanup follows Delete downloads when done; with it off, downloaded audio is kept. Automatic downloads remain a separate setting.
- Volume Boost and silence trimming are intentionally not advertised in these notes.
- No shared CloudKit schema or signing settings change is included.
- Michael authorized the App Store release on 2026-09-09 after the build 263 device/TestFlight work. Automated checks do not establish physical VoiceOver focus behavior for the new Unfollow control. See `docs/folder-run-implementation.md` for the folder-run acceptance history.
