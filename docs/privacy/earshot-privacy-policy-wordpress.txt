<p><em>Effective August 8, 2026</em></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Earshot is an accessibility-first podcast player provided by Payown Media LLC. This policy applies specifically to the Earshot application.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The short version: Earshot does not collect information about you. There is no Earshot account, no advertising, and no tracking. Your library can sync between your devices through your private iCloud account; Payown Media LLC cannot access that private database.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":2,"anchor":"what-we-collect"} -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="what-we-collect">What Earshot collects</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Earshot collects nothing for Payown Media LLC. Specifically, Earshot does not collect for Payown Media LLC, use for tracking, or share:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Your identity. Earshot has no account or login.</li>
<li>Your podcast subscriptions, listening history, queue, folders, bookmarks, or settings. These stay on your devices and in your private iCloud account; Payown Media LLC cannot access them.</li>
<li>Analytics or usage data. Earshot contains no analytics SDK.</li>
<li>Crash reports. Earshot contains no third-party crash-reporting service.</li>
<li>Advertising identifiers. Earshot has no advertising and does not access an advertising identifier.</li>
<li>Information for cross-app or cross-website tracking. Earshot contains no third-party trackers.</li>
</ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":2} -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Where your data is stored</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Your podcast subscriptions, listening history, queue, folders, bookmarks, playback position, and shared preferences are stored locally and can synchronize through Apple's CloudKit service in your private iCloud account. Synchronization is automatic when iCloud is available. Payown Media LLC does not operate a sync server and cannot read your private CloudKit records.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Downloaded audio, cached artwork, purchase entitlement state, and download preferences remain device-local and are not synchronized through CloudKit. Deleting Earshot from one device ordinarily removes that device's local data, but does not by itself delete a synchronized library from iCloud or your other devices. Earshot provides separate actions to clear only this device or to delete the synchronized library everywhere.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":2} -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Network connections Earshot makes</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Earshot connects to the internet to provide podcast-player functionality. Depending on the actions you take, Earshot may connect to:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Podcast publishers and hosting providers to retrieve podcast feeds, episode audio, transcripts, chapters, and artwork.</li>
<li>The public podcast directory when you search for podcasts.</li>
<li>Apple services when Earshot retrieves product information, processes purchases, or verifies purchase status through StoreKit.</li>
<li>Apple's CloudKit service when Earshot synchronizes your library through your private iCloud account.</li>
</ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Those requests go directly to the relevant service. As with any internet connection, the service may receive technical information needed to respond to the request, such as your IP address. CloudKit uses your Apple account to provide your private database; Earshot does not send that account identity to Payown Media LLC. Podcast and search requests do not include an Earshot account identifier, advertising identifier, or tracking identifier. Each external service is responsible for its own privacy practices.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":2} -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Purchases</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Earshot Plus subscriptions, the lifetime unlock, and optional tips are processed by Apple through StoreKit. Payown Media LLC does not receive your payment-card details, billing address, Apple ID, or complete StoreKit transaction history.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Earshot checks purchase and subscription status on your device so it can provide the features you purchased. Earshot stores the resulting entitlement status locally and does not upload it to Payown Media LLC.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Apple's handling of purchase information is governed by <a href="https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/">Apple's Privacy Policy</a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":2} -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Optional feedback</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Earshot can help you compose a feedback email. Sending feedback is optional. You can review and edit the message before sending it through your email provider. If you send an email, Payown Media LLC receives the information you choose to include, such as your email address and message. That information is used to respond to your request and provide support.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":2} -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Children</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Earshot does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Earshot has no user accounts, advertising, analytics, or tracking.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
