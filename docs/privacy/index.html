Earshot Privacy Policy

Effective July 15, 2026

Earshot is an accessibility-first podcast player. The short version: Earshot collects nothing about you. There is no account, no sign-in, and no tracking. Everything you do in the app stays on your device.

What Earshot collects

Nothing. To be specific, Earshot does not collect, store off your device, or share:

  • Your identity. There is no account and no login.
  • Your subscriptions, listening history, queue, folders, bookmarks, or settings. These live only on your device.
  • Analytics or usage data. There is no analytics SDK in the app.
  • Crash reports. There is no crash reporter in the app.
  • Advertising identifiers. There are no ads and no ad IDs.
  • Third-party trackers. There are none.

What stays on your device

Your subscriptions, listening history, queue, folders, bookmarks, and settings are saved locally on your device. They are not uploaded to Payown Media or anyone else. If you delete the app, that data goes with it.

Network connections Earshot makes

Earshot connects to the internet only to do the things a podcast player has to do, and only with servers you choose by following a show:

  • Downloading podcast feeds and episode audio from the show's own host.
  • Fetching show artwork.
  • Searching the public podcast directory when you use search.

These requests go directly to those services. Their own privacy practices are their own. Earshot does not add any identifier of you to these requests.

Purchases

Earshot Plus subscriptions, the lifetime unlock, and optional tips are processed entirely by Apple through StoreKit. Payown Media does not receive your payment-card details, billing address, Apple ID, or StoreKit transaction history. Earshot checks purchase and subscription status on your device so it can grant the features you bought. It stores only the resulting entitlement state locally and does not upload it to Payown Media.

Apple's handling of purchase information is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

Earshot does not collect data from anyone, including children, because it does not collect data at all.

Changes to this policy

If this ever changes, the change will show up on this page with a new effective date.
