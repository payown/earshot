# Earshot Apple Watch: one-session VoiceOver device test

This guide is for the integrated internal TestFlight candidate after its build number is recorded in `docs/watchos-progress.md`. It is **not** evidence that hardware checks have passed. Use a paired iPhone and Apple Watch with VoiceOver enabled. Record the build number, watch model/watchOS, iPhone/iOS, and whether the watch uses Wi-Fi, cellular, or neither.

For each step, note **Pass** or **Fail**, the exact words VoiceOver spoke, what audio you heard, and where focus landed. A short screen recording or sysdiagnose is useful only when a result fails.

1. **Install and launch independently.** Install Earshot on Apple Watch from TestFlight, then turn the iPhone off or take it out of range. Open Earshot on the watch. Expected: Home opens without waiting for iPhone, and Library, Inbox, Queue, Downloads, Bookmarks, Settings, and Now Playing remain navigable. If possible, repeat after removing the iPhone Earshot app while leaving the watch app installed.
2. **Browse and subscribe.** In Library, dictate or enter a known HTTPS podcast feed. Open the show, browse episodes, and read show notes with VoiceOver. Expected: Subscribe reports success or a specific error; each row says whether it is played or downloaded; double-tapping its Play control starts watch playback; Details is a separate control. Check a folder if your account has one. Try an invalid feed and confirm the error is spoken.
3. **Stream on watch.** With the iPhone still unavailable, play one episode over Wi-Fi. If the watch has cellular, turn off Wi-Fi and play another. Expected: Now Playing identifies Apple Watch as the playback device, reports title, elapsed and remaining time, and names the audio route. The episode continues when you lower your wrist or leave Earshot.
4. **Use playback controls.** With VoiceOver, pause and play, restart, skip back and forward, adjust the Playback position slider with swipe up/down, change speed, and move between chapters where offered. Set a 15-minute sleep timer, then turn it off. Expected: one action causes one clear result; slider value includes minutes and seconds; speed selection is announced; chapter and timer controls have text labels. Confirm the Digital Crown still performs VoiceOver navigation normally.
5. **Download and play offline.** From Episode details, download a short episode. Check Downloads for progress and completion. Turn on Airplane Mode, make sure the iPhone is unavailable, relaunch Earshot and play that file. Expected: audio plays locally; no phone or network prompt is required. Cancel a second download and confirm no partial file appears as playable. Delete a completed download; focus should move to the Downloads heading. If watch storage is nearly full, verify a readable error and no false success.
6. **Manage listening state.** In Inbox, switch between New and All Unplayed, play, mark played, and check that focus returns to its heading. In Queue, add an episode with Play Next or Play Later, move it, remove it, and check focus. Add a bookmark in Episode details, open it from Bookmarks, and delete it. Expected: state survives app relaunch and a watch reboot.
7. **Use Bluetooth and interruptions.** Connect Bluetooth headphones, play audio, disconnect them, and then reconnect. Expected: Earshot pauses when the output disappears, names the route, and does not unexpectedly resume. Interrupt with Siri or a call if available; expected: interruption is acknowledged and playback can be resumed deliberately.
8. **Control and transfer.** Start audio on iPhone, open iPhone Control on watch and refresh its state. Pause, resume, and skip the phone from watch. Transfer phone playback to watch; then transfer back from watch Now Playing. Expected: the interface names the actual device, the receiving device starts before the source stops, and one device remains playing. Take the phone out of range during a control or transfer attempt; expected: a spoken failure and unchanged source playback, with no false success.
9. **Check sync conflicts.** On both devices, make different progress, played/unplayed, Queue, bookmark and follow edits while separated. Reconnect, open Settings > Synchronize now on watch, and reopen both apps. Expected: deliberate seek backward and later queue removal remain respected; an unresolved subscription conflict is visible instead of being silently discarded. Note both devices' final states and the order of edits.
10. **Use complications and Shortcuts.** Add Earshot's circular, rectangular and inline complications where the face permits; add it to Smart Stack. Activate each. Expected: Earshot opens Now Playing; stale episode text eventually falls back to Earshot. Try Open Now Playing, Resume, Pause, Skip Forward and Skip Back shortcuts. On iPhone, choose an episode under Settings > Apple Watch Downloads and confirm that the watch receives a request and downloads directly.
11. **Review accessibility settings.** Repeat a pass through Home, Library, Inbox, Queue, Downloads and Now Playing with the largest text setting, high contrast, Reduce Motion and button shapes. On a smaller watch if available, confirm text is scrollable and every action remains reachable. Expected: no unlabeled controls, unexpected focus jumps, clipped essential status or gesture-only action.

When something fails, send the step number, build number, device/OS versions, exact action sequence, VoiceOver speech, expected versus actual audio or state, and whether the iPhone, Wi-Fi, cellular and Bluetooth were available. This is enough to start reproduction without another broad test session.
