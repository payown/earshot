Chapter 61, Build 193

The same position, without reopening

What changed

Earshot now refreshes an open paused player from the position that iCloud saved,
even when the player still holds an older in-memory episode. The Now Playing
position and its existing VoiceOver value update without relaunching the app.

This build also includes build 192's honest iCloud status, bounded older-episode
paging, durable refresh results, and lock-time feed-refresh fix.

What to test

Leave Now Playing open on one device. Play and pause the same episode on the
other device, then confirm the new position appears without relaunching. Test an
offline queue change across force quit and reconnect. On a large podcast, load
older episodes 10 at a time and confirm VoiceOver focus remains stable.

The physical-device heat issue is already confirmed resolved and does not need
another timed run unless the phone becomes warm or VoiceOver responsiveness
regresses.
