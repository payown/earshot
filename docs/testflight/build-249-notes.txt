Chapter 79, Build 249

The shelf without a membership card

What changed

Discovery is now a place where you can stay and listen, not just a short stop
before following a show. Open an unfollowed podcast to browse its available
episodes, search episode titles and descriptions, and sort newest or oldest
first. Each episode can play immediately or be added to and removed from the
queue. None of those actions follows the podcast or downloads its episodes.

If you later follow that podcast, Earshot promotes the same catalog record into
your Library instead of making a competing copy. Queue membership, played state,
listening position, and other local state remain attached. Large OPML imports
also continue reporting progress as they stream into the Library.

Build 249 fixes the crash reported from build 248. If playback finishes or
removes an episode while iCloud is applying queue changes, reconciliation now
works from plain snapshots and commits through the app's current data context.
The completed episode should stay gone instead of returning or leaving a stale
relationship that SwiftData can crash on.

What to test

Find a podcast you do not follow. Browse far beyond its newest five episodes,
search for a word from an older title or description, and switch between newest
and oldest. Play an episode, add it to the end of the queue, remove it, and add
it again. Confirm the podcast remains unfollowed until you explicitly choose
Follow. Then follow it and confirm the queued episode and listening position
survive in the Library.

With iCloud sync active, finish a queued episode while moving or removing other
queue items. Let sync complete, close Earshot, and reopen it. The finished
episode should not return, queue order should remain sensible, and Earshot
should not crash. If you use two devices, repeat while the other device changes
the queue.

Kashe opened a show a client had mentioned and found three years of episodes.
She searched for the guest's name, put one interview at the end of her queue,
and left the Follow button alone.

The interview finished between visits while her other device caught up. That
evening she opened Earshot again. The episode was still finished. The show was
still not in her Library. Both were exactly where she had left them.

Renata asked whether she had found a new podcast.

“I found one episode,” Kashe said. “I don't need a membership card for the
whole shelf.”
