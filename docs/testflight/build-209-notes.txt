Chapter 66, Build 209

Playback handoff now explicitly checks your private iCloud database before the
same episode resumes on another device, carrying both the newest position and
playback rate. Offline or unavailable iCloud never blocks local playback, and
pending pause, seek, rewind, or rate changes retry after reconnection.

The existing opt-in cleanup setting is now called Delete downloads when done.
One switch removes a download after you finish or mark an episode played, remove
it from the queue, or clear the queue. It remains off by default. Queue removal
still does not mark the episode played, and automatic queue limits do not delete
downloads.

What to test

On two devices using the same iCloud account, play and pause an episode on
device A after changing the playback rate. Resume that episode on device B and
confirm its position and rate. Repeat from B to A with a rewind and a skip
forward. Make one change offline, close Earshot, reconnect, and confirm the
offline change eventually wins. Playback should still begin promptly if iCloud
is unavailable.

In Settings, Downloads, enable Delete downloads when done. Download a queued
episode, remove it from the queue, and confirm it leaves Downloads without being
marked played. Disable the setting, repeat, and confirm the download stays.

Build 209 also retains build 206's private-iCloud listening-history crash
repair. Open, background, and return to Earshot several times while iCloud is
active. If Earshot closes unexpectedly, submit every crash through TestFlight
and tell us the approximate time, device, episode, and action. Repeated crash
logs are essential: the log itself lets us distinguish a new failure from the
crash already repaired.

Kashe paused the interview on her phone before opening the Mac. Earshot checked
the small note in iCloud before the second voice spoke: four minutes and six
seconds, one and a half speed, the same breath continued in another room.

Later she abandoned a downloaded panel discussion. She removed it from the
queue and found the file gone too—not because Earshot had called it finished,
but because she had said she was done. Her listening ledger remained honest.

“The space between them,” Kashe said. “It finally remembers.”
