disabled and undeployed. Sync measurements in this record used only the
development environment of `iCloud.media.payown.earshot`.

- Podcasts: 100.

- Queue items: 10, with all keyed identities and order preserved.
- Listening sessions: 3.
- Bookmarks: 0.

- Downloaded files: 292; failed downloads: 8.

## Migration and initial projection

VoiceOver announced migration steps 1, 2, and 3, then “Earshot is ready.” The
app opened Inbox with 287 episodes. Migration completed no later than 29.720
seconds after process start. The exact development projection-seed duration was
not recoverable later because its informational unified-log markers had already
expired; the durable projection transaction spanned 0.155 seconds. Development
CloudKit setup took 1.531 seconds, import 0.441 seconds, and the primary export
4.260 seconds.

The clean post-upload V10 copy passed integrity for the primary, local, and
compact-projection stores. It contained:

- Primary: 100 podcasts, 27,755 episodes, 10 queue items, 3 sessions, 0
  bookmarks, 5 mirrored settings, and Inbox 287.
- Projection: 100 podcasts, 6 episode-state rows, 10 queue rows, 5 setting rows,
  0 bookmarks, 3 sessions, and 0 folders.
- CloudKit metadata: 124 records and 0 pending uploads.

## Two-device proof

The Designed-for-iPhone Mac began empty and imported 100 subscriptions, 10
queue episode shells, 3 listening sessions, and the preserved settings. Its feed
refresh reported completion but materialized no catalog beyond queue shells, so
the Mac ended with 11 episodes and Inbox 0. This is tracked by #821.

- Phone to Mac queue: 10 to 11. “Trusting medicine in an age of doubt” from TED
  Radio Hour arrived.
- Mac to phone queue: 11 to 10. Removing the MEDIA BUZZmeter item reflected on
  the already-open phone.
- Phone to Mac playback: Tuesday Topics moved from 40 to 273 seconds.
- Mac to phone playback: the Mac exported 379 seconds. The open phone continued
  exposing 274 seconds, then exposed 379 after force-quit and relaunch. This is
  tracked by #825.
- Mac to phone setting: skip back changed from 15 to 30 seconds and reflected
  live.
- Phone to Mac setting: global speed changed from 1.75x to 2.0x and reflected
  live.
- Inbox dismissal: no independent action exists. “Mark as Played” also removes
  the episode from Inbox, and the projection has no dismissal field. This is
  expected 1.2 work tracked by #824, not a 1.1 migration failure.

At the final light snapshot, both compact projections had identical counts: 100
podcasts, 9 episode states, 22 per-device queue contribution rows, 8 setting
rows, 0 bookmarks, 8 sessions, and 0 folders. All six copied SQLite stores
returned `ok`. Final effective queue count was 10 on both devices. Tuesday
Topics was 388 seconds on the phone and 387 seconds on the Mac.

Skip forward ended at 60 seconds on both devices. Projection timestamps identify
an explicit phone-originated setting write at 19:03:19 PDT; the post-migration
copy had already proved that migration preserved 45 seconds. This is a Phase 6
interaction, not migration loss.

## Release conclusion

The build-155 V5 to build-186 V10 migration gate passed with 27,755 episodes.
The ordinary Release configuration has
`EARSHOT_DEVELOPMENT_CLOUDKIT_ENABLED=NO`; it does not create, seed, or open the
compact projection. Production CloudKit was not touched.

Deferred to the Earshot 1.2 milestone:

- zero-work feed refresh reporting success (#821);
- live playback-position UI refresh (#825);
- independent Inbox dismissal and forward sync (#824);
- retained development seed timing markers (#823);
- offline/reconnect and playback heat samples (#820);
- account-change behavior (#814); and
