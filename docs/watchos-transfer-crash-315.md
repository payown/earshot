# Watch transfer crash on build 315

Michael reported that transferring an actively playing episode from iPhone to Apple Watch closes EarshotWatch without transferring audio. VoiceOver Magic Tap on the Watch still controls iPhone playback. Turning Show App on Apple Watch off and on did not change the transfer failure. This report is distinct from the build 311–312 **iPhone** crash when merely opening the Watch app.

Three physical Watch reports from November 8, 2026 at 21:40:07, 21:41:02 and 21:48:25 Pacific identify **EarshotWatch 1.2.6 (315)** on **watchOS 27.2 (24S118)**. All report `EXC_BREAKPOINT / SIGTRAP` on a utility operation queue with the same Watch Connectivity reply stack. The reports contain device information and remain outside the repository. Their arm64 app UUID `F0CB5D72-EC3A-3D18-96ED-E2349F79B584` matches the signed build 315 archive and its dSYM. Symbolicating app offsets 849780 and 832528 against that dSYM yields `closure #1 in WatchPhoneControl.requestHandoffState(library:watchPlayer:)` and its escaping callback thunk. This establishes the failing callback; the dispatch and Swift Concurrency frames make an actor/executor assertion the likely mechanism.

The `sendMessage` reply ran on Watch Connectivity's utility queue but its unannotated closure was created inside a `@MainActor` class. The fix makes the reply handler explicitly `@Sendable`, parses its immutable source state off the main actor, then delivers it to main-actor UI and library code. The following pause and resume reply handlers also now declare `@Sendable`; the pause handler captures an immutable episode identity instead of a non-Sendable SwiftData model. The phone playback and handoff protocol are unchanged.

The focused Watch simulator test invokes the production reply handler from a utility queue and verifies delivery on the main actor. It passed, followed by the full Watch suite: **47 unit tests and 5 UI tests, zero failures**, on a watchOS 27.2 simulator. These tests do not establish physical transfer success. After a new authorized internal build, update both apps in place and repeat the transfer at normal and 1.5x, checking that neither app closes, the Watch starts near the iPhone's final position, the phone stops only after confirmation, and VoiceOver announces one accurate result. Capture exact time and diagnostics for any recurrence.

Start Testing issue: `6a93fde4-e2f9-4aee-ac97-b3b9f536da4d`. This fix is code-tested and awaits physical acceptance; no new build has been uploaded. The separate paused-transfer limitation remains: the button and source parser currently require phone audio to be playing.
